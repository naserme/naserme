Approach

How I work

I focus on understanding the requirement, making a practical change, and checking how it behaves in the actual product.

I usually work on existing products: understanding a request, identifying the part of the system it affects, implementing the change, and checking it before release. My work includes business features, APIs, payment integrations, database work, and production troubleshooting.

I also build supporting tools when a product needs them. Recent work includes an internal crawler for competitor and market data, and messaging bots connected to a financing platform. My regular tools include PHP, Laravel, Slim, MySQL, SQLite, Redis, Linux, Nginx, Supervisor, and Git.

I use AI-assisted development tools as part of my workflow, but I review the changes, run tests, and verify behavior myself before treating the work as complete.

Financial technology

May 2025 – Present

Since May 2025, my work has covered the development and maintenance of several financial products, starting with gold-trading platforms and later moving to a crowdfunding platform and its supporting tools.

Gold-trading platforms

I started by maintaining and extending two existing products: a retail platform for users to buy and sell gold from 1 mg, and a wholesale platform for gold-trader partners with a 100 g minimum transaction size.

Crowdfunding platform

I later moved to the company’s crowdfunding product, which has around 9,100 registered users. My work includes developing a customer-loyalty area for the marketing team, managing payment gateways, and delivering smaller product improvements.

Internal tools & integrations

Built a crawler from scratch, with guidance from the senior IT lead, to collect data from Farabourse and competitor websites. AI-assisted extraction results are presented in Apache Superset for a team of around 15. I also developed Telegram and Bale bots for the crowdfunding platform’s users.
